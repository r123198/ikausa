Questions

Good to know.

What is Ikausa?

A shared film camera for one event. You start a roll, your guests join it from their own phones, and everyone shoots on the same film look. The photos develop together at the time you choose.

Do my guests need to download an app?

No. Guests scan your QR code or open your link, and the roll opens on their phone.

Does it work on Android?

Yes. Guests can join from iPhone or Android. Hosts can start a roll on the web today, and the iPhone app is coming soon.

When can people see the photos?

You pick: right away, the moment the roll closes, or 24 hours after it closes. Until then, nobody peeks, not even at their own shots.

Who can see the album?

Everyone on the roll, once it develops. If you'd rather keep it close, set the album so only you, the host, see everyone's photos.

How do I keep the photos?

Open the roll after the reveal and save it. On a phone the photos go straight to your camera roll; on a computer you get a zip file. The original of every shot is kept alongside the film version.
