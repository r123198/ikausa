How it works

Three steps. Then the best part: waiting.

  1. 01

    Load the roll

    Name your event, set when it closes and pick a film look. Then design the invite card your guests will see first.

  2. 02

    Pass the camera

    Share a QR code or a link. Guests join from their own phone and shoot until the roll closes.

  3. 03

    Let it develop

    Photos stay hidden until the reveal. Then everyone gets the whole roll, from every angle of the night.

The film

Pick one look. Everyone shoots on it.

Every photo on the roll gets the same film, so the album feels like one night instead of forty different phones. The original is always saved too.

Warm, punchy colour with green-tinted shadows and grain, like a drugstore film camera.

The reveal

You decide when the film develops.

  1. Right away

    Everyone sees photos as soon as they’re taken.

  2. When the roll closes

    Photos stay hidden and develop the moment the roll closes.

  3. 24 hours later

    Photos develop the day after the roll closes, like real film.

Keeping it close? Set the album so only you, the host, see everyone’s photos.
