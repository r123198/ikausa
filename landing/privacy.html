1. Who we are

Ikausa is operated by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], [BUSINESS ADDRESS] (“we”, “us”). We are the personal information controller for the information described here.

For anything about your data, contact our Data Protection Officer at [PRIVACY EMAIL].

2. What we collect

When you make an account

  • Your email address and password, or, if you sign in with Google, the name and email address on your Google account. Passwords are stored only in hashed form.
  • The display name you choose, and a profile photo if you add one.

When you join a roll as a guest

  • The name you type in. We create a guest account for you that has no email address or password.

When you host or join a roll

  • Roll details: its name, start and end times, film look, reveal and download settings, cover photo and invite card.
  • Every photo taken on the roll, both the film version and the original, along with who took it and when.
  • When you try to join rolls, so we can stop people guessing invite codes.

When you pay

  • Which pass or shot pack you bought and a transaction reference. In-app purchases are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your card details.
  • If you pay us directly by GCash, the amount and the reference number you give us. We never see your GCash login.

Automatically

  • Standard technical logs kept by our hosting providers, such as IP address, browser or device type, and the time of a request.

We use your camera only to take photos for a roll, and your photo library only when you pick a cover or profile photo or save a roll. We don't ask for your location or your contacts.

3. How we use it

  • To run Ikausa: sign you in, create rolls, apply film looks, reveal photos at the right time and let you save them.
  • To show your name next to the photos you took, so people on the roll know who shot what.
  • To process purchases and give you the passes and shots you paid for.
  • To keep the service safe, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To reply when you contact us.

We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we don't use your photos to train AI models.

4. Who can see your photos

  • The host picks when photos are revealed: right away, when the roll closes, or 24 hours later. Until then, nobody on the roll can see them, not even their own.
  • Once revealed, everyone on the roll can see all of its photos, unless the host sets the roll so only the host sees everyone’s photos.
  • Rolls are private. They are never public and can only be joined with the roll’s invite code or link, so anyone the host shares it with can join.
  • A small number of our staff can access rolls when needed to provide support, investigate abuse or meet a legal obligation.

5. Services we rely on

We share information with these providers only so they can run parts of Ikausa for us:

  • Supabase: our database, photo storage and sign-in.
  • Vercel: hosts this website and the web app.
  • Google: sign-in with Google, if you use it, and the fonts on this website.
  • Apple and Google: app stores and in-app payments.
  • RevenueCat: keeps track of in-app purchases so we can unlock what you bought.

These providers may store information on servers outside the Philippines. We only use providers that protect it to a standard comparable to the Data Privacy Act. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

6. How long we keep things

  • Photos: each roll has a download window of 7, 30 or 90 days, counted from when the roll closes or its photos are revealed, whichever is later. Photos and originals are permanently deleted within about 7 days after that window closes, so save the ones you want to keep.
  • Your account: kept until you delete it.
  • Purchase records: kept as long as tax and accounting laws require.
  • Logs: kept for a limited time by our hosting providers.

7. Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes:

  • your account and profile, including your profile photo
  • every photo you took, on any roll
  • the rolls you host, including all their photos
  • your remaining passes and shot packs

This can’t be undone. If you can’t sign in, email [PRIVACY EMAIL] from the address on your account and we’ll delete it within 30 days. If you joined as a guest, email us the roll’s name and the name you used, and we’ll delete your guest account and photos.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your information is used
  • get a copy of your information, and receive it in a usable format
  • correct information that is wrong
  • object to processing, or have your information deleted or blocked
  • be compensated for damages caused by misuse of your information

To use any of these rights, email [PRIVACY EMAIL]. If you’re not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.

9. Children

Ikausa is not meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you’re under 18, use Ikausa only with a parent’s or guardian’s permission. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

10. Storage on your device

The app keeps you signed in by storing a sign-in token on your device. We don’t use advertising or analytics cookies, on this website or in the app.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we’ll also tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
